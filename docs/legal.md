# Legal Notice

## Authorized Use Only

RedScope is a penetration testing tool designed for **authorized security testing only**.

You **must** have explicit written permission from the system owner before scanning any target. Unauthorized security scanning may be illegal in your jurisdiction, regardless of intent.

!!! danger "Unauthorized Scanning is Illegal"
    Running RedScope against systems you do not own or lack explicit written authorization to test may violate:

    - Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) — United States
    - Computer Misuse Act — United Kingdom
    - Türk Ceza Kanunu Madde 243-245 — Türkiye
    - And equivalent laws in most countries worldwide

## Intended Users

RedScope is intended for:

- Penetration testers with written authorization from their clients
- Security researchers testing systems they own or control
- Bug bounty hunters operating within the defined scope of a program
- Students learning security concepts in lab environments (HackTheBox, TryHackMe, VulnHub, etc.)
- Organizations performing authorized internal security assessments
